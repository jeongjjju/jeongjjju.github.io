Sign language interpretation services through an interpreter are limited, so a sign language translator using artificial intelligence can help communication with people who are deaf or hard of hearing.

In this paper, a portable finger sign language translation device that can be connected to a mobile phone is designed and implemented. The device translates finger motions and outputs them as speech, then recognizes the other person's voice and displays it as finger animations.
